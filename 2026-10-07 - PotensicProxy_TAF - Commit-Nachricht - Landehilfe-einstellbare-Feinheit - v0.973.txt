BL_Add adjustable landing-assist sensitivity
Date: 2026-10-07

Summary:
- Added a persistent, non-centering landing-assist sensitivity slider with a 10-25 percent range and a 15 percent default.
- Added direct percentage input and live display of the resulting fine-control axis limit.
- Grouped Throttle, Yaw, Pitch and Roll into a visually separate fine-control section while keeping their self-centering behavior.
- Clamp active fine-control axis values when the configured sensitivity is reduced.
- Bumped the WebUI and Android application version to 0.973 / versionCode 46.
- Added PDF and DOCX project documentation plus delivery notes for the change.
